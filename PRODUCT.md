# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Written from the owner's build brief and one round of answers (October 2026).

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Three.js, React Three Fiber, drei, postprocessing, TanStack Query. Chosen by the owner; the same stack as their Rotom Dex. Static deployment (Vercel), no backend.

## Users

People deciding whether to work with Otis: internship supervisors, studios and recruiters, teachers and fellow developers. They arrive from a link in an application or a GitHub profile, on a desktop at work or a phone in between things, and want to know within a minute who Otis is, what Otis has built and how to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Otis van der Stelt's portfolio. It shows who Otis is, what Otis can do and has made, and how to write, and it is itself the strongest piece of evidence: a hand-built 3D world instead of a page of cards. Success is a visitor who explores more than one landmark and then opens a project or writes a letter.

## Positioning

The site is a place, not a document. Sections are landmarks on one small map (a hill, a greenhouse, a train station, a windmill meadow, a postbox) and moving between them is a camera flight, not a page load. Successor to the owner's Next.js bento-grid portfolio at otisvanderstelt.vercel.app.

## Operating Context

Opened cold, often once. Many visits are on a phone, so everything must be reachable there without the free-roaming camera. No sound. Works without any network call succeeding: project data ships with the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five landmarks: Home (hill and signpost), About (greenhouse in the roots of an oak), Projects (train station, one project per train car), Skills (windmill meadow, one windmill per category), Contact (postbox, a letter that is a form, charms that are social links).
- Desktop: free camera with damped orbit and pointer parallax. Touch or low-power devices: fixed camera angles as animated backdrops, a bottom text menu, and text sheets that slide up.
- The world is night after 19:00 by the visitor's clock (fireflies, dark theme); a header toggle switches sun and rain.
- Contact has no server: the letter opens the visitor's own mail app, addressed to otisstelt@outlook.com.
- 3D assets are generated by scripts in the repo and shipped as Draco-compressed `.glb` files.
- Undecided: the three projects on the train. The owner asked for placeholders for now; they live in `public/data/projects.json` and are labelled as placeholders on screen.
- Undecided: the LinkedIn profile URL (the charm is a placeholder until the owner supplies it).

## Brand Commitments

Pinned by the owner's brief:

- The world is inspired by Studio Ghibli's pastoral films: hand-painted warmth, whimsy, nostalgia. All assets are original; nothing is copied from a film.
- Cel shading with ink outlines. Textures like watercolour bleeds, paper tooth and brush strokes.
- Palette: antiqued cream and oatmeal canvas as bases; moss green, clover, rustic terracotta and soft cornflower blue as accents. Eternal golden hour with long soft shadows.
- The interface must feel analog, soft and organic; never sharp, corporate or digital.
- Labels are hand-written text, not icons: skill labels, social charms, the mobile menu and the weather toggle are all words.
- Motto, in Otis's own words: "Break things, learn from them, build better."

## Product Principles

- The world is the navigation. Anything that can be a landmark or an object is not a menu.
- Everything readable is real HTML text over the scene, never pixels inside it.
- The phone version is the same world seen from fixed viewpoints, not a different site.
- Never fake content: what the owner has not supplied is shown as a labelled placeholder.
- It has to stay light enough to load on a phone connection: generated geometry, compressed models, the 3D code in its own chunk.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every landmark and its content reachable by keyboard and by screen reader without the 3D scene; visible focus; 4.5:1 body text contrast in both the day and night themes; `prefers-reduced-motion` replaces camera flights with cuts and stills the ambient motion; touch targets at least 44 px.
